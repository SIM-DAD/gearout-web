License terms

GearOutSIM DAD LLCA summary. The full agreement comes with every quote and before any evaluation starts.

What an institution signs

A purchase is an Order Form plus the GearOut End User License Agreement. Managed hosting adds a Data Processing Agreement under the FERPA school-official exception, because SIM DAD operates the stack that holds the records. For a self-hosted image or sealed media the Data Processing Agreement is optional, because no student record reaches SIM DAD.

An evaluation runs under a separate Evaluation Agreement: thirty days at most, no fee, test data only. Continued use past the end date requires a signed End User License Agreement.

What the license grants

  • Use by the institution's own students and staff, for the institution's own equipment checkout desks.
  • Delivery in the way the Order Form says, with updates and support from SIM DAD LLC.
  • Backup copies for the institution's own operation.

The license does not permit sublicensing, redistribution, or running the software as a service for another organization.

Your data, your branding

Data the institution enters stays the institution's. The institution's branding and configuration are not derivative works of the software, so nothing the institution sets up becomes anyone else's property.

Source code

The agreement does not entitle an institution to source code. Where SIM DAD provides source to a self-hosted institution for its own operation, it does so under the agreement's confidentiality clause.

Terms required by the licensor

GearOut was developed at Southern Illinois University Edwardsville and is licensed exclusively to SIM DAD LLC by the Board of Trustees of Southern Illinois University. The agreement carries terms the licensor requires in every customer agreement, and those terms cannot be changed by either party. Everything else, from fees and payment mechanics to governing law for claims against a public institution, is written to be edited by the institution's counsel.

Warranties and liability

Warranties and support come from SIM DAD LLC alone. The full text states SIM DAD's warranty, its disclaimer, and the limits on each party's liability.

Getting the full text

Write to ap@simdadllc.com. The End User License Agreement, the Evaluation Agreement, and the Data Processing Agreement are sent with every quote, so the institution's counsel reads them before anything is signed.
