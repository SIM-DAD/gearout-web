Equipment checkout for media programs

Run the equipment cage from a phone and a scanner, on a stack your institution controls.

GearOut gates checkout by the course roster, runs as one installation per institution, and is priced per program at a published price: not per administrator seat and not by quote.

What it does

For students

  • Reserve from a phone: pick blocks on the calendar and confirm from the bottom bar.
  • Show a 6-character pickup code or a QR pass at the desk. The pass opens offline.
  • Get the emails that matter: the confirmation, a reminder at 5:00 PM the day before, a waitlist offer when a slot opens.

For the desk

  • Scan either direction. A gear label or a student's pass resolves to check out or check in with one button.
  • See who has what, what is overdue, and what is due today on one board.
  • Approve renewals, record damage, settle fines, and open any student's profile from any table.

For the department

  • Eligibility follows the course. An instructor launches GearOut from Blackboard and the roster syncs through LTI 1.3; every enrolled student is eligible. No central-IT directory sync. Roadmap: Canvas
  • Rules you set: schedule blocks, a weekly limit of three time slots per student, fines, holds, waitlists with 24-hour offers, production briefs.
  • Reports: utilization, overdue, fines, and per course, with CSV export.
  • Your branding: institution and department names, colors checked for contrast, your official logo.

For campus IT and the accessibility office

  • One stack per institution. Your data on your stack: self-hosted, on sealed media, or as an appliance. PostgreSQL row-level security, secrets encrypted at rest, TLS in front.
  • Sign-in through your identity provider: Microsoft Entra ID, Google Workspace, or SAML 2.0 (Shibboleth, ADFS, Okta) through SSOReady.
  • Built to WCAG 2.1 AA. A VPAT 2.4 report is in preparation ahead of the April 26, 2027 DOJ Title II deadline. Accessibility status
  • No AI feature touches student data. Nothing calls home. Small footprint: 2 vCPU, 4 GB RAM, one install command.

Three facts to check us on

  1. Roster gating the department turns on itself. GearOut is an LTI 1.3 tool. An instructor launches it from the course in Blackboard, the roster syncs through Names and Role Provisioning Services, and every enrolled student becomes eligible.
  2. A published, flat price per program. The Department tier is $1,788 per year. The lowest published price at the closest higher-education competitor is about $6,624 per year, because it is priced per administrator with a three-administrator minimum (sources).
  3. One stack per institution. The institution's data lives on its own stack, which it can host itself, receive on sealed media, or take as an appliance. No student data passes through SIM DAD.

Questions a buyer asks

How many universities use it?
One, in production during 2026. GearOut 2.0 is the version built for other institutions. We would rather be honest about the count than pad it.
Do you have SOC 2?
No. GearOut is built so it does not need to hold your data: the self-hosted, sealed-media, and appliance options keep every record on your stack. The managed option is SIM DAD running one stack for you, never a shared database. Security
Does it work with our identity provider?
Entra ID, Google Workspace, and SAML 2.0 (Shibboleth, ADFS, Okta) through SSOReady. Your identity team registers one redirect URI. Roadmap: verified against a campus Shibboleth IdP
Does it work with our LMS?
Blackboard today through LTI 1.3 with roster sync. Rosters can also be uploaded as a CSV with a preview before anything is written. Roadmap: Canvas adapter
Is there a mobile app?
GearOut is an installable web app. Students add it to the home screen, the pass opens offline, and there is no second app-store surface to keep accessible.
Can we buy it on a card?
The Starter and Department tiers plus any provisioning fee stay under common purchasing-card thresholds. The Campus tier with the appliance option is the one combination that can cross into full procurement, and it is scoped for that.
