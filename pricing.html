Pricing

Prices are published, flat, and per program. Not per administrator seat, not per item, not by quote. Billed annually, by invoice or on a purchasing card. Prices ratified September 18, 2026.

Delivery, one time

Delivery options and one-time provisioning fees
Delivery optionOne-time feeWhat you get
Managed (hosted) Included in the subscription A web address. SIM DAD runs one stack for you, never a shared database.
Self-hosted image (configure and validate) $750 Your instance, configured to your LMS, sign-in, domain, roster model, and branding; validated end to end on an empty instance; delivered digitally with a client-IT handoff note.
Sealed media $900 The same validated instance on encrypted physical media, for air-gapped or download-averse environments.
Appliance $1,500 plus hardware at cost A pre-built, validated small-form-factor machine shipped ready to connect. Hardware typically $400 to $700, itemized.

Every delivered instance is validated empty. You load student data only after acceptance, so nothing of your students' ever passes through SIM DAD.

What is not on this sheet

  • No per-administrator seats. Add as many desk staff as you have.
  • No student caps on Department and Campus.
  • No setup fee for managed delivery.
  • No AI add-on, because there is no AI in the product.
  • Training, custom connectors, and multi-year terms: [owner to set].
