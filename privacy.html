Privacy

1. This website

This site keeps an aggregate daily count of page views through a first-party counter. The counter sets no cookie, stores no IP address, and is skipped when your browser sends a Do Not Track signal.

2. The early-access form

The form on the early-access page sends your email address, and the name and note if you add them, to a SIM DAD LLC service hosted on Cloudflare, which emails the request to us through Brevo, our email provider. We use the address to reply about the evaluation and for nothing else. It joins no mailing list, and we do not share it with anyone else. Write to ap@simdadllc.com to have it deleted.

3. The mailing list

Joining the mailing list on the release-news page is consent to receive GearOut marketing email from SIM DAD LLC. We keep the address on the GearOut list, use it only for those emails, and never sell or share it. Every email carries an unsubscribe link, and a reply with the word "unsubscribe" works too; we honor a request within 10 business days and keep the address only on a do-not-email list afterward, so we do not email it again.

4. A GearOut installation

GearOut runs as one stack for one institution. SIM DAD receives no telemetry and no usage data from an installation, and nothing in the software reports back to us. Data crosses the stack's boundary only for the purposes the installation serves:

  • The institution's identity provider, for sign-in: email, display name, and the attributes the institution releases.
  • The learning management system, through LTI 1.3: launch claims and, for roster sync, the course membership.
  • The institution's own email relay, for the notices a student gets about their reservation.
  • A certificate authority, when the installation uses Let's Encrypt: the public hostname.
  • The SSOReady broker, only when the institution chooses SAML sign-in.

No data from an installation is sent to an advertising network, an analytics service, or an AI service.

Whether it arrives as a self-hosted image or on sealed media, the institution holds the server, the keys, and the backups, and SIM DAD has no standing access. The full hosting and data-flow statement, which lists every flow by delivery option, is sent with the security packet.

5. Data retention

The page counter keeps only daily totals. An early-access request is kept until it has been answered and the evaluation has ended, or until you ask for it to be deleted. A mailing-list address is kept until you unsubscribe. Data inside an installation is retained according to the institution's own policy; the software never deletes it on its own.

6. Changes

If this page changes, the effective date above changes with it. The current version is always at this address.
