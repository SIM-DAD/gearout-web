Privacy

This site sets no cookies, loads no third-party scripts or fonts, and stores nothing in your browser.

What this site collects

  • An aggregate daily page count: the day, the site name, and the page path, added to a counter. No address, no identifier, no user agent, nothing that can be tied to a person.
  • The counter is skipped entirely when your browser sends Do Not Track.
  • Email you send us is handled like any business correspondence and used only to reply.

What the GearOut product collects

The product is installed per institution and holds what an equipment desk needs: a student's name, email, and course enrollment, plus reservations, fines, and holds. It holds no payment card data and no government identifiers. The hosting and data-flow statement, the security summary, and the FERPA school-official data processing agreement template describe this in full; see Security for the document list.
