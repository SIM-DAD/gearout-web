How to read it

GearOut's lane is narrow and specific: roster-gated lending for a media program at a published price, on a stack the institution controls. WebCheckout and Connect2 can gate by course, but through a directory or SIS integration that central IT owns, and neither publishes a price. Cheqroom publishes a price per administrator seat, which punishes the multi-staff cage a media program runs. Snipe-IT is free and excellent at IT assets, and does not do date-bound student reservations. LibCal Lending Hub is the one to watch at any campus that already owns LibCal.

What GearOut does not have: a customer count, a SOC 2 report, native mobile apps, and a Canvas adapter (Roadmap). We say so.
